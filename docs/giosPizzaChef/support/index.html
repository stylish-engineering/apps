Support

Thanks for using Gio Pizza, the pizza dough calculator with tested recipes and a step-by-step guide.

Contact

For questions, bug reports or recipe suggestions, email engineering.meets.style@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem. We usually answer within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app need an account or an internet connection? No. Everything works offline and there is nothing to sign up for.

Where are my recipes stored? On your phone only. Recipes you create under "Mine", your settings and your step progress are saved in the app's local storage and are deleted together with the app. Nothing is uploaded.

How do I change the number of pizzas or the ball weight? On the recipe card, tap "Dough Balls" or "Ball Weight" and use the number pad. All ingredients are recalculated immediately.

Why does the yeast amount change when I change the room temperature or fermentation? Yeast works faster when it is warmer and when it has more time. The calculator adjusts the yeast (or sourdough) quantity so the dough is ready when the step-by-step guide says it is.

Can I use instant, active dry or fresh yeast? Yes. In the "Step by Step" guide, step 1 lets you pick the yeast type, and the amounts are converted for you. The Neapolitan Sourdough recipe uses a sourdough starter instead.

The timer notification did not appear. Check that notifications are allowed for Gio Pizza in Settings → Notifications, and that Focus mode is not silencing them. The timer itself keeps running inside the app.

How do I reset the step-by-step progress? Open "Step by Step" and tap "Reset" in the top-right corner.

Is the app really free? Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.
