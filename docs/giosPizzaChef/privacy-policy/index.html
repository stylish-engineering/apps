Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

Gio Pizza ("the app") is a pizza dough calculator and step-by-step guide developed and operated by Stylish Engineering (Josué Gonçalves). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. It does not connect to any server operated by us, and it does not send any information about you or your device anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of data locally on your phone so it can work offline and remember your choices:

  • your app settings (for example measurement units and room temperature),
  • the recipes you create or copy under "Mine",
  • the progress of the step-by-step guide and any running timers.

This data never leaves your device. It is stored in the app's private storage and is removed when you delete the app. You can also reset it at any time from within the app.

Notifications

If you start a dough-rise timer, the app may ask for permission to show local notifications so it can alert you when the timer ends. These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device; no push-notification service or server is involved. You can revoke this permission at any time in your phone's Settings.

Links to other websites

The app may contain links to our website (pizza.doughyourbest.com) or to recipe videos. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read. Opening a link is always your choice; the app does not open external pages by itself.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above, and we will describe the change in the App Store release notes.
