Privacy Policy — Mister Eleven

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Mister Eleven ("the app") is an online football manager game, developed and operated by Stylish Engineering. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: we keep only what you need to sign in and play, we show no ads, we use no analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.

Data we collect

Your sign-in. You sign in with Apple or Google; there is no other way to create an account. From that sign-in we receive and keep:

  • your email address (if you choose "Hide My Email" with Apple, we only receive Apple's private relay address),
  • your name, if Apple or Google shares it (with Apple, only if you choose to share it the first time you sign in),
  • the identifier Apple or Google gives your account, our own account identifier, and the dates you signed up and last signed in.

We never see or store your Apple or Google password.

Your game. To run the game we store what you create and do in it:

  • your teams (name, crest and kit), players, line-ups, tactics, training and youth academy,
  • the leagues you create or join, matches, results, statistics, cups and awards,
  • transfer market bids and listings, and your club's money records (in-game money only; there are no real payments),
  • press releases, comments, likes and chat messages you post, and any manager you block or content you report,
  • your settings (language and which inbox notifications you want), and when you last used the app, which the game's rules use to treat inactive managers as away.

Why we use it

  • Your sign-in data lets you sign in and keeps your teams yours. Your email address is used for nothing else: we send no newsletters or marketing.
  • Your name is shown as your manager name in your leagues.
  • Your game data is the game itself: we need it to play your matches, run your leagues and show you and the other managers the results.
  • Technical logs (below) are used only for security and to fix problems.

Who can see it

  • Your email address is never shown to other players. You see it yourself in Settings.
  • Your name, teams and game activity are visible to the managers in your leagues, as the game requires: for example in standings, match reports, the press and chat.
  • Chat messages are read only by the members of the room they were posted in (a league room, or a direct conversation between two managers).
  • League admins and we can remove reported content. We read reported content only to deal with the report.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The app shows no advertising, contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for marketing.

Where your data is stored

Your account and game data are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Supabase processes the data only to provide this service to us. Like any online service, its servers keep short-lived technical logs, such as sign-in events and the IP address and time of requests, for security and troubleshooting.

Sign-in itself goes through Apple or Google, under their own privacy policies.

Data stored on your device

So the game still opens without a connection, the app keeps a copy of what the server has already shown you, and any line-up or tactics changes waiting to be sent. Your sound setting is kept on the device too. This copy is removed when you sign out, when you delete your account, and when you delete the app.

The app asks for no special permissions: no location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

How long we keep it

  • Chat messages are deleted after 90 days. A reported message is kept with its report until we have dealt with it.
  • Read inbox notifications and other technical records are deleted after a limited time.
  • Everything else is kept while your account exists, because it is your game.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → General → Delete account. When you confirm:

  • your sign-in identity (email address, name and account identifier), your settings, your inbox and your private game records are deleted,
  • your teams finish the current season and are then retired, or leave their league at once, as the confirmation screen explains,
  • the chat messages, press posts, comments, likes and reports you made, and your clubs' money records, stay in the leagues' history so the game stays consistent for the other players, but your name is removed from them and they are no longer linked to any person.

Deletion cannot be undone. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address you signed in with and we will delete the account for you.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it, by emailing the address below. If you are in the European Union, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Mister Eleven is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Test versions

Before the public release, the app is tested with a small group of invited players. Test accounts and game data may be deleted when the testing ends.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above, and we will describe the change in the release notes.
