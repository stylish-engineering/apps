Support — Mister Eleven

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Thanks for playing Mister Eleven, the online football manager game. Run your club in a private league with your friends: pick the line-up and tactics, train your players, buy and sell on the transfer market and play for the league and the cup.

Contact

For questions, bug reports or ideas, email engineering.meets.style@gmail.com. If you are reporting a problem, please include your phone model, its system version and, if you can, your team's name. We usually answer within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in? With your Apple or Google account. There is no separate password to remember.

How do I play with my friends? One of you creates a league in the app and shares its code. The others enter the code to join and create their teams.

Does the app need an internet connection? Yes, the game runs on our server. Without a connection you can still look at your club and change your line-up and tactics; the changes are sent when you are back online.

Is the app free? Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. The money in the game is in-game money only.

How do I delete my account? Open Settings, then General, and tap "Delete account". The confirmation screen tells you what happens to your teams. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address you signed in with.

Someone is posting abusive messages. Report the message or post in the app, and block the manager if you want. You can also email us.

Privacy

The app shows no ads and does no tracking. Read the full privacy policy here: Privacy Policy.
